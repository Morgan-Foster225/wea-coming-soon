Our Mission

WE1A, Inc. provides trauma-informed, structured, accountability-based support and emotional skills development designed to assist individuals in achieving long-term stability and successful community reintegration.

Our Vision

We envision communities where reentry, recovery, and personal growth are supported through structured frameworks that promote accountability, opportunity, and lasting transformation.

Our purpose is to reduce barriers to successful reentry by delivering trauma-informed, accountability-based support and structured services. We focus on emotional stability, workforce readiness, and long-term reintegration.

As we grow, we are opening our doors to additional populations who can benefit from structured guidance, connection, and trauma-informed care. While our foundation is rooted in supporting justice-impacted women, our mission has always been centered on people. Today, we are honored to support anyone seeking a safe, nurturing space for healing, growth, and transformation.

Through strategic partnerships with correctional institutions and the phased development of structured residential programming, WE1A is committed to reducing recidivism and strengthening long-term community outcomes.
